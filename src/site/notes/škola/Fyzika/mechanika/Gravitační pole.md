#### Vzorečky:$$
\begin{gathered}
F_\text{g}=G\cdot{}\frac{m_1\cdot{}m_2}{r^2}
\\\\
a_\text{g}=\frac{G\cdot{}M_{z}}{r^2}
\\\\
g=a_\text{g}-a_\text{d}\vphantom{\frac{M}{M}}
\\\\
v_1=\sqrt{\frac{G\cdot{}M_{\text{z}}}{R_{\text{z}}}}
\\
v_2=\sqrt{2}\cdot{}v_1\vphantom{\frac{M}{M}}
\\\\
T=\frac{v_0}{g}\\h=v_0\cdot{}t-\frac{1}{2}\cdot{}g\cdot{}t^2
\\
H=\frac{v_0^2}{2\cdot{}g}
\\
v=v_0-g\cdot{}t\vphantom{\frac{M}{M}}
\end{gathered}
$$
